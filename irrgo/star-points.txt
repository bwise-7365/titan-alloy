On rectangular grids, there are some star points drawn. This is done in BoardWidget.cpp around the comment "If large enough, draw center star point"
If there is a center point, then add four more star points (dots on the grid) on the top, bottom, left and right.
On the left and right sides, they have the same row as the two other star points but the col of the center.
On the top and bottom side, they have the same col as the two other star points, but the row of the center.
