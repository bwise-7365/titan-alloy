GRAPHICAL USER INTERFACE

Again, use Qt 6.8

(*) The overall layout has a menu bar at the top and an IrrGo board below it. To the right is an area for various controls. At the bottom of the control area
is a scrollable text output widget. Each move will be displayed there as it is made. Keep a record of the moves made in a game, as they will be saved later.
Each Move object, in C++ terms, is a record of the turn number, then either PASS or the (color, row, col) of the move.

The top-left entry of the standard menu bar should be "File", with operations Save, Save As .. and Load. Those operations will be explained later.

The size of a board, whether it is rectangular or irregular, is specified by two integers, M and N,
nominally rows and columns. For a rectanglular board, draw a grid with M rows and N columns.
For an irregular board, generate a random planar graph of M*N nodes; the generation process is defined later.
Every GameNode object is a point on the board and has (row, col) attributes. The row and column are
specified when the graph is first built; see below.

(*) The graph is drawn with a dot at each node and a thin line connecting it to its neighbors in the graph.
The color of the dots, lines and frame are #373C65. There should be a menu to select
the background color of the board. The choices are #DBAD6b (the default), #FFD169, #0C7F84.
A pale wood-grain background with a mix of colors, like ash or walnut, is optional.
Black stones are solid discs of #000000 and White stones are solid discs of #FFFFFF.

(*) On a rectangular board, the grid lines are not quite matched to the size of the stones.
The standad diameter of a stone is 7/8, or 0.875 inches.
The standard spacing is 0.87 inches for width (seperation between columns) and 0.93 for length (seperation between rows).
This forces the stones to be slightly off-center, in random ways, to slightly disrupt the perfect symmetry of the grid.

(*) The board-definition menu will offer a drop-down menu of sizes: 7 x 7, 7 x 9, 9x9, 9x13, 13x13, 15x15, 15x17, 17x19, 19x19, 21x21.
It will have a checkbox for "Irregular", initially unchecked, with an integer numeric entry box titled "Seed".
There will be a menu for "Stones" with mutually exclusive choices for Empty, Sparse, Medium, Dense, or Solid.
They are defined by the number of stones to be placed, as a fraction of the number of points on the board.
The fractions are 0%,  20%, 40%, 75%, or 100%. Place the stones one at a time, in alternating order starting with Black,
according to the rules. That is, no stone may be placed on another and captures are performed. During this setup phase,
the super-ko rule is not applied. Thus, even when a stone is placed on every point, the resulting position will have Empty
points because of captures.
The board is generated and optionally populated with stones when the player clicks the last entry in the menu, "Generate".
It will use the provided random number, so that it produces the same board until the user changes the random seed.

(*) On any graph, I want to highlight Empty nodes whenever the user mouses over them. Make the sensitive region 1/2 the diameter of a stone.
If it is White's turn to move, draw a White disc of the regular size, but with an orange edge. Make the thickness of the edge be 1/10 of the diameter.
If it Black's turn, draw a Black disc with orange edge.
On irregular graphs, I want
to highlight the incident edges of that node in medium purple at the same time.

When the user mouses over an occupied place (Black or White), display a red disk on the stone to indicate
that it would be an illegal placement. Do not accept moves that attempt to place a stone in an occupied place.

(*)  Left clicking on an Empty node places a stone of the players color, cross-hatched in grey. Left clicking again
within 3 seconds confirms the placement, otherwise it disappears, the stone is not placed, and it is still the player's move.
There are two boxes to the side of the board for passing moves. One is marked "White Pass" and performs a Pass move for White.
The other is marked "Black Pass" and performs a Pass move for Black. Each is enabled on that player's move and disabled on the
other players move.

(*) There will be a "Play" drop-down menu in the bar. The entries should be
"Manual Placement", "10 Seconds", "30 Seconds", and "60 Seconds". For now, they do nothing.
