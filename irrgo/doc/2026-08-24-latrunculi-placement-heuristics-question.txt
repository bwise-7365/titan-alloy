The opening 'placement' phase of the Ludus Latrunculorum presents a problem.
It is typically about 40 moves long, with no obvious negaMax static evalution.
MCTS doesn't help because of the endless shuffling: almost all 'random play' rollouts end in stalemate.
So I want you to analyze the rules and develop some heuristics for how to place stones in the initial phase,
before movement starts. From ancient sources, I know that one tactic is to build a horizontal or vertical wall.
Two others I have observed in play is to build a diagonal or to build a solid 2x2 square (cannot be captured).
Also, one can place a "guard" near the walls, diagonals, or squares. Finally, placing a stone in the corner
or *not* placing stones where they can be pinned in one move. So my info-only (don't change anything yet)
question is whether you can analyze the rules, think through the interactions, and recommend heuristics
(things to do and things to avoid) that could be implemented in this C++ to play the opening on
anything from 6x6 to 10x12 boards.
