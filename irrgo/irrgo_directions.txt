I want to change the 'staticEval' function for IrrGo's negamax (Do not change it for MCTS).
To do that, first define a new class for "Discrete Voronoi Region" called DVR.
The discrete Voronoi region for Black is all those nodes on the grid that are closer to a Black stone than to any White stone.
This includes nodes that hold a Black stone, because those are at distance zero.
The discrete Voronoi region for White is all those nodes on the grid that are closer to a White stone than to any Black stone.
Again, this includes nodes that hold a White stone.
The nodes at equal distance are not in either DVR.
The DVR class should take an irrgo board, a color, and an integer radius. 
It should return the DVR on that board of that color, but only out to the radius.
For the Black color, a radius of 1 means only those points directly connected to a Black stone but not directly connected to any White stone..
If you need clarification, ask; otherwise proceed.


The new static evaluation function for negaMax is as follows. White's score does have komi added.
The 'dvrScore' of a side is the total size of its DVR. Define a constant in the function called 'areaPremium' with value 1.01
The 'stoneCount' of a side is the number of stones they have on the board.
The 'territoryScore' of a side is (1 + areaPremium)*dvrScore - areaPremium*stoneCount.
