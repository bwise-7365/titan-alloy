/plan

I have modified LogisticalAdjudicator to reset TheVRC and TheIB from the provided ScenarioRecord. 
I want to create TEntity and SEntity that are placed at the nodes, with the names, of all the Transport and Serial objects
defined by the ScenarioRecord. I need to maintain association between the TEntity and SEntity. In one direction, e.g. from TEntity to Transport, it is already done because the TEntity has a Transport data member, myTrans. Similar to SEntity. In the other direction, I need Map<Serial, SEntity> and Map<Transport, TEntity> that are updated by the constructors of SEntity and TEntity when the entities are created. That ensures the two-way association stays consistent.

I want you to propose a design so that the TEntity and SEntity can do several crucial steps.

(*) The SEntity has four states, Select, Wait, Move and Delivered.
Select means it should select a transport backlog (using SerialController.selectBacklog) and join it, then enter the Wait state.
The Wait state means it is in a backlog and is literally waiting for the transport to add it to an itinerary and start its own
TEntity.Move state. When the transport starts to Move, the SEntity enters the SEntity.Move state and stays there. It leaves the Move state and enters either a Select or Delivered state. If the Serial is at its delivery node, it enters the Delivered state, then does not schedule or respond to any more Events. If it is not at its delivery node, it enters the Select state.

(*) The TEntity has three states, Plan, Transfer, and Move.
At the start of the simulation, and when a TEntity finishes its Itinerary and moves to its home base, it enters the Plan state.
In the Plan state, it runs every X hours (that should be a constant, not a hard-coded value, as I will want to experiment with values like 0.5 or 3.0 hours) to see if there are Serials in its backlog. If so, it should use ItineraryBuilder.itineraryFromBacklog to build a backlog. Then it starts to perform the Itinerary.

(*) To perform an Itinerary, the TEntity must keep track of which Itinerary.Leg it is on and whether
it is at the 'src' or 'dst' Itinerary.Stop of that Leg. If it is at a 'src', then doTransfer will create and record
a TransportRecord for "load" "start" of each Serial on its transfer Manifest for that Stop. This will occur at the transferSrtTime of that Stop. It has to schedule another EntEvent for the transferEndTime of that 'src' Stop.
When that event is processed, the TEntity should enter the Move state and schedule an event for the dst.transferSrtTime,
which is when it arrives at the next node. It creates a TransportRecord for the start of unload at the dst.node,
and schedules another event for the transferEndTime. When that event is processed, it creates a TransportRecord for the
finish of unload of all the serials in the transfer Manifest of the dst node. All those Serials (i.e. their SEntity) also
enter either Delivered or Select, depending on whether they were dropped off at their destination or not.
Finally, if a TEntity is killed while in the Move state, every serial on it at that time is also killed.
