Billing and Subscription Information                                                                                                                 
   Whether you are billed for each interaction depends on your chosen authentication method. Junie offers three ways to manage usage and costs:         
                                                                                                                                                        
   1. JetBrains AI Subscription                                                                                                                         
   If you are logged in with your JetBrains Account, Junie is included as part of your JetBrains AI subscription.                                       
                                                                                                                                                        
   • Billing: You are not billed per interaction. Your usage is covered by your monthly or annual subscription fee.                                     
   • Limits: Usage is subject to the quotas of your plan. If you reach these limits, the service may be restricted until the next cycle, but you will   
   not incur unexpected charges.                                                                                                                        
                                                                                                                                                        
   2. Junie API Key Usage-Based                                                                                                                         
   If you use a JUNIE_API_KEY configured via junie.jetbrains.com/cli (https://junie.jetbrains.com/cli):                                                 
                                                                                                                                                        
   • Billing: This is a pay-as-you-go model. You are billed based on the number of tokens input and output used in each interaction.                    
   • Control: You can monitor your balance and set spending limits on the Junie website.                                                                
                                                                                                                                                        
   3. Bring Your Own Key BYOK                                                                                                                           
   If you provide your own API keys for providers like Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google:                                                                    
                                                                                                                                                        
   • Billing: JetBrains does not bill you. You are billed directly by the LLM provider according to their specific pricing.                             
                                                                                                                                                        
   How to Stay Within Your Budget                                                                                                                       
   To manage your costs and ensure you stay within your subscription or balance, you can use the following commands within the Junie interface:         
                                                                                                                                                        
   • Check Current Usage: Run /usage to see the token count and cost breakdown for your current session.                                                
   • Verify Account Status: Run /account to see which authentication method is currently active.                                                        
   • Model Selection: Use /model to ensure you are using the Default model optimized for cost/quality or a model covered by your specific subscription. 
                                                                                                                                                        
   For most users with a JetBrains AI subscription, staying logged in via your JetBrains Account is the best way to ensure no new charges are incurred  
   beyond your regular subscription fee.
