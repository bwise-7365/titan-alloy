One standard outline of an academic research article is the following:

1. Introduction

    Introduces the research topic
    States the research question or hypothesis
    Provides background information and context

2. Literature Review

    Reviews existing research related to the topic
    Identifies gaps in the current knowledge
    Justifies the need for the current study

3. Methodology

    Describes the research design and approach
    Details the methods used for data collection and analysis
    Discusses the sample size and selection criteria

4. Results

    Presents the findings of the research
    Uses tables, figures, and charts for clarity
    Summarizes key data without interpretation

5. Discussion

    Interprets the results in the context of the research question
    Compares findings with previous studies
    Discusses implications, limitations, and potential for future research

6. Conclusion

    Summarizes the main findings
    Restates the significance of the research
    Suggests recommendations or future research directions
