Main sections of a technical report

Section	Description
Title Page	Includes the report title, author(s), affiliation, and date of submission.

Abstract	A concise summary of the report, highlighting objectives, methodology, key findings, and conclusions.

Table of Contents	Lists all major sections and sub-sections with corresponding page numbers for easy navigation.

Introduction	Provides background information, states the report's purpose, and outlines the problem being addressed.

Methodology	Describes the research or analysis methods used, including data collection and experimental procedures.

Results	Presents the findings of the research, often with tables, graphs, and figures for clarity.

Discussion	Interprets the results, discussing their implications and how they relate to the research question.

Conclusions	Summarizes the main findings and their significance, often including recommendations.

References	Lists all sources cited in the report, ensuring proper attribution of information.

Appendices	Contains supplementary material that supports the report but is not essential for casual readers, such as raw data or detailed calculations.
